# Decision Brief

A decision brief should contain:

1. Direct answer in two to four sentences.
2. Governed metrics with definitions and owners.
3. Largest affected customers, products, orders, and contracts.
4. Causal evidence paths.
5. Ranked actions with owner, organization, due date, and rationale.
6. Assumptions, unresolved questions, ontology version, and generation time.

Use plain business language. Do not obscure definitions or caveats. Keep the mitigation tracker operational and assign one accountable owner per action.
