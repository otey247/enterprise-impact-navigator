# Facilitator Demo Script

## Opening

"Most enterprise questions are not blocked by a lack of data. They are blocked because every system describes the enterprise differently. This demonstration shows an ontology acting as the shared operating layer between those systems."

## 1. Fragmented enterprise

Open **Overview** and call out:

- ERP, procurement, CRM, finance, contracts, inventory, and organization sources
- Supplier names that differ across systems
- Finance and Sales definitions of revenue at risk
- Pending mappings requiring governance

## 2. Run the disruption

Select **30-day supplier disruption**.

Ask:

> If Nova Components' Monterrey facility is unavailable for 30 days, what is our exposure?

Call out confirmed revenue, Sales exposure, gross margin, contractual penalties, affected products, plants, customers, and orders.

## 3. Trace the evidence

Select `SO-1001` and show:

```text
Nova Components
  → Monterrey site
  → PART-1048
  → Industrial Flow Controller
  → SO-1001
  → Acme Industrial
  → Strategic Supply Agreement
  → Operations owner
```

## 4. Resolve semantic disagreement

Run **Why do Sales and Finance disagree?** Explain that Finance counts confirmed revenue while Sales adds qualified pipeline. The ontology retains both definitions and their decision contexts.

## 5. Govern the ontology

Open **Mappings**. Review `Nova MX`, `Nova Components Group`, and `P&G`. Approve or reject a mapping and show that the decision is retained for the demo session.

## 6. Move to action

Review owners and due dates, then export the decision brief and mitigation tracker.

Close with:

"The graph is not the product. The product is a governed, evidence-backed decision that crosses enterprise boundaries."
